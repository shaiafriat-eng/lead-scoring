What it is

A method to rank leads by demographic fit (A–D) and behavioral engagement (1–4), combined into codes like A1 or B3.

Why it exists

Prioritize sales outreach, reduce MQL noise, and route leads using conversion data—not treating every 100-point MQL equally.

How the score works

Two dimensions—fit and engagement—combine into a score code used for prioritization and MQL routing.

Demographic (A–D)

Person (function, seniority) + Account (ICP, status, geo, EE count) → grade A–D.

Behavioral (1–4)

Activities earn Marketo points → bucketed into tiers 1 (100+) through 4 (0–14).

Systems: Marketo (scoring & MQL), Salesforce (ICP), 6Sense (intent & booth bonus). MQL threshold: 100 points.

Dimension 1 · Fit

Demographic score (A–D)

A Best fit

ICP + decision maker/champion (incl. 50–99 EE when ICP flag lags).

B Strong fit

ICP + relevant function, or non-ICP + decision maker.

C Workable

Non-ICP workable persona or ICP validating function.

D Lowest / DQ

Irrelevant fit, junk, bad geo, competitor, customer, EE out of range.

ICP definition

An account is ICP when it meets size, technology, industry, and geography rules—or is manually overridden in Salesforce.

Account must meet all criteria below (unless overridden):

  • 100–5,000 employees
  • At least one modern technology in tech stack
  • Industry is NOT colleges/universities, government, federal, or schools
  • US-based companies must be international
  • OR manually flagged via ICP override field

Disclaimer: We may still MQL accounts with 5,000–8,000 employees under current policy, even though they fall outside the standard ICP employee range.

Score reduction

When the behavioral score is reduced

These statuses reset Behavioral Score Calculation in Marketo so leads don’t stay above MQL after they should stop being worked.

ScenarioScoreWhy
Blacklisted country → 0 The person or account is in a restricted / high-risk country. Engagement should not keep the lead above MQL thresholds.
Not Relevant → 0 The Salesforce account is marked Not Relevant. Sales should not work the lead, so behavioral points are cleared.
Unsubscribe → 0 The person opted out of email. Continuing to score engagement would misrepresent intent and risk compliance issues.
Inactivity → 0 No meaningful engagement for 3 months — no form submission, no LGF, and the behavioral score has not changed. Stale points are cleared so old activity cannot keep the lead MQL-eligible.
Nurture → 0 Sales parked the lead in Nurture. Behavioral score is reduced to 0 by default.
Nurture reason · Migration → 10 Mid-migration / not ready to buy — score is set to 10 so the lead is not auto-MQLd again immediately.
Nurture reason · Unresponsive → 10 Lead stopped responding — score is set to 10 until they re-engage.
Nurture reason · Changes in personnel → 20 Buying contact changed — score is set to 20 so the account can resurface with a new stakeholder.
Nurture reason · No internal capacity → 20 Timing is wrong (no bandwidth) — score is set to 20 rather than wiping intent entirely.

After reduction, the lead must earn new points before reaching the 100-point MQL threshold again.

Qualification issues

Common MQL Qualification Issues

Employee Count Discrepancies

Company size data is often inaccurate, mainly due to reliance on ZoomInfo without Apollo as a secondary source.

Job Title Classification

Most title-related issues involve broad roles such as student, intern, sales, or marketing.

WAD vs. Scoring MisalignmentWIP

Some WAD completions have strong titles but receive a low score due to company attributes, preventing MQL qualification.

Conversions on Not Relevant Accounts

Leads converting on accounts already marked as not relevant are not assigned to sales and lose MQL status.

FAQ

What is lead scoring?
A structured way to rank leads by fit (demographic A–D) and engagement (behavioral 1–4) so Marketing and Sales prioritize outreach and MQL routing based on expected conversion—not a single point total alone.
What is the MQL point threshold?
Leads typically need 100+ points in the Behavioral Score Calculation field to reach MQL-eligible engagement tiers, combined with demographic grade and channel-specific auto-MQL rules.
How is the behavioral score calculated?
Activities earn points in Marketo (Behavioral Score Calculation field). Point totals map to tiers: 100+ → 1, 50–99 → 2, 15–49 → 3, 0–14 → 4. Scores update as people engage.
Can email clicks alone create an MQL?
No. Email link clicks (+5, max 3× per month) cannot reach the 100-point MQL threshold by themselves.
Why wasn't my lead MQL'd?
Common blockers: grade D or junk/DQ flags, points below 100 without a qualifying channel combo, activity-based paths limited to A1/B1 only, WAD exclusions (e.g. D3), excluded sources (ROI calculator, New Movers asset), or booth rules (only A + Decision/Purchase bonus). See Why not MQL. If you still believe the lead should have MQL'd, follow Manual MQL review.
How do I request a manual MQL review?
Run the self-check in Why not MQL, gather lead IDs, scores, source, and recent activities, then submit to Marketing Ops / RevOps via your team’s standard intake. See the Manual MQL review section for the full checklist and outcomes.
How are junk or fake leads identified?
Leads are screened for test/fake data, invalid emails, QA/UAT records, restricted countries, failed qualification, and low-quality email signals—with exceptions for approved domains and reviewed records.
Why didn't every ICP booth attendee become an MQL?
After a 2023 pilot overload, only top booth attendees qualify: demographic A plus 6Sense Decision/Purchase stage receive a +35 bonus (with attendance points) to surface the top ~5% of booth traffic.
Why does ICP sometimes show FALSE for new contacts?
Salesforce ICP can take ~24 hours to calculate. Marketo ICP scoring is used interim until SFDC updates.
Can a lead score decrease?
Yes. Behavioral Score Calculation is reduced in specific ops scenarios: Blacklisted country, Not Relevant, Unsubscribe, Inactivity (no form submission / LGF / score change for 3 months), or Nurture → 0 by default. Specific nurture reasons override that — Migration or Unresponsive → 10; Changes in personnel or No internal capacity → 20. Demographic grade (A–D) can also worsen when account or persona data changes. After a reduction, the lead must earn new engagement before reaching the 100-point MQL threshold again. See MQL Policy → When the behavioral score is reduced.

Methodology & limitations

  • Behavioral tiers and point buckets describe the same dimension.
  • ICP and score updates have operational lag.
  • Demographics are rule-based, not ML.
  • Confirm live rules in Marketo before major campaigns.
