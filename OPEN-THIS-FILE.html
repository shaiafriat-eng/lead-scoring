Understand HiBob lead scoring

Fit, engagement, and priority in one view.

Our two-dimensional model helps Marketing, Sales, and RevOps focus on leads most likely to become sales-qualified opportunities.

What it is

A method to rank leads by demographic fit (A–D) and behavioral engagement (1–4), combined into codes like A1 or B3.

Why it exists

Prioritize sales outreach, reduce MQL noise, and route leads using conversion data—not treating every 100-point MQL equally.

Mechanics

How the score works

Demographic (A–D)

Person (function, seniority) + Account (ICP, status, geo, EE count) → grade A–D.

Behavioral (1–4)

Activities earn Marketo points → bucketed into tiers 1 (100+) through 4 (0–14).

Systems: Marketo (scoring & MQL), Salesforce (ICP), 6Sense (intent & booth bonus). MQL threshold: 100 points.

Routing

MQLing flow

How fit, engagement, and channel rules combine before a lead becomes an MQL. ICP is the account-level gate for demographic scoring.

ICP definition

An account is ICP when it meets size, technology, industry, and geography rules—or is manually overridden in Salesforce.

Account must meet all criteria below (unless overridden):

  • 100–5,000 employees
  • At least one modern technology in tech stack
  • Industry is NOT colleges/universities, government, federal, or schools
  • US-based companies must be international
  • OR manually flagged via ICP override field

Full rules and edge cases: maintained by MIS.

Dimension 1

Demographic score (A–D)

A Best fit

ICP + decision maker/champion (incl. 50–99 EE when ICP flag lags).

B Strong fit

ICP + relevant function, or non-ICP + decision maker.

C Workable

Non-ICP workable persona or ICP validating function.

D Lowest / DQ

Irrelevant fit, junk, bad geo, competitor, customer, EE out of range.

Junk & test lead criteria

Classified as junk if one or more apply:

  • Test/fake data in names, company, notes, or email (e.g. test, asdf, Lorem Ipsum)
  • Invalid, disposable, or bounced email
  • Internal QA/UAT from non-production environments
  • Restricted/high-risk countries (Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Sudan, Libya, Iraq, Afghanistan, Somalia, Pakistan, India, China, Palestine, Lebanon, etc.)
  • Fails qualification (e.g. missing referral form)
  • Flagged via operational junk-lead processes
  • Low-quality email validation (free/disposable indicators)

Exceptions for approved companies, domains, referral programs, and reviewed records.

Exceptions

Common reasons a lead won't be MQL'd

Reaching 100+ points or high engagement is not enough—fit, channel, and March 2025 combo rules all apply.

Fit & account disqualifiers

Grade D, bad geo, competitor/customer, EE out of range, irrelevant persona.

  • Demographic D: junk, Not Relevant status, restricted countries, Update MQL Process = FALSE (<20 or >8,000 EE)
  • Unsubscribed, HiBob employees, Sales job function, student/intern titles

Engagement below threshold

Under 100 points or low-intent activity only.

  • Tiers 3–4 (typically 0–49 points) without a qualifying hand-raise or WAD path
  • TOFU-only; email clicks (+5, max 3/mo) cannot reach 100 points alone

Channel & score-code rules

March 2025 limits which codes auto-MQL by source.

  • Activity-based: only A1 & B1 auto-MQL
  • WAD: no D3; C3 only selective regions (no Micro)
  • No auto-MQL: ROI calculator, High-value asset (“New Movers”)
  • Hand raiser forms always MQL when valid (demo, pricing, contact sales)

Junk, test & data quality

Screened before or regardless of points.

  • Test/fake data, invalid/bounced email, QA/UAT records
  • Failed qualification, ops junk flags, disposable email signals

Events & booth (common misconception)

ICP booth attendance alone does not MQL everyone—only A demographic + 6Sense Decision/Purchase get the +35 bonus (~top 5%). Attendance (+15) still requires passing DQ checks and channel combo rules.

Quick check before escalating

Confirm grade (not D), lead source, score code vs auto-MQL list for that channel, and junk/DQ flags. See MQL policy, scoring flow, or manual MQL review if it still looks wrong.

Escalation

Manual MQL review

When a lead looks qualified but did not MQL, use this path before assuming automation failed. Threshold: 100 points + channel rules.

When to request a review

  • High-intent action (demo, pricing, contact sales, WAD) but no MQL in expected timing
  • Score code or demographic grade seems wrong for the account/persona
  • ICP, EE count, or account status recently changed—may not have synced
  • Source/campaign should qualify under March 2025 policy but was excluded

Before you escalate

Most “missed” MQLs are policy-intentional. Confirm first:

  • Why not MQL
  • MQL policy (2025)
  • Scoring flow
  • MQLing flow (ICP)

Review process

  1. 1
    Self-check against policy

    Use Why not MQL and MQL policy—confirm exclusion was intentional (e.g. D3 on WAD, not A1/B1 on activity path).

  2. 2
    Verify data freshness

    Allow ~24h for SFDC ICP; confirm Marketo flows ran since last activity.

  3. 3
    Open review with Marketing Ops / RevOps

    Use your team’s standard Slack channel or ticket queue. Include the checklist below.

  4. 4
    Reviewer decision

    Manual MQL, data/rule fix and re-run flows, confirmed no MQL, or documented one-off exception.

Support

FAQ

What is lead scoring?
Fit (A–D) + engagement (1–4) for prioritization and MQL routing—not points alone.
What is the MQL point threshold?
100 points in Behavioral Score Calculation, plus demographic grade and channel-specific auto-MQL rules.
Can email clicks alone MQL someone?
No. +5 clicks, max 3/month, cannot reach the 100-point threshold alone.
Why wasn't my lead MQL'd?
See Why not MQL. Still wrong? Follow Manual MQL review.
How do I request a manual MQL review?
Self-check policy, gather IDs/scores/activities, submit to Marketing Ops / RevOps via your team intake. Full checklist: Manual MQL review.
How are junk/fake leads identified?
Test/fake data, invalid emails, QA/UAT, restricted countries, failed qualification, ops junk flags, and email quality signals—with approved exceptions.
Why not all booth attendees MQL?
Only A demographic + 6Sense Decision/Purchase get +35 booth bonus (~top 5%).
ICP shows FALSE for new contacts?
SFDC ICP can take ~24h; Marketo ICP used interim.

Trust

Methodology & limitations

  • Behavioral tiers and point buckets describe the same dimension.
  • ICP and score updates have operational lag.
  • Demographics are rule-based, not ML.
  • Confirm live rules in Marketo before major campaigns.
